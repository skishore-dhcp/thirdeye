About us

ThirdEye is a brand identity governance platform created for CodeNexus India 2026, brought to life by a one-person team.

We at ThirdEye aim to assist startups and growing organizations with low capital and resources to smoothen and streamline their marketing process, especially the consistency of brand identity. Any informal email, company document with errors, or a prototype flyer - we review it all, in accordance with your brand.

We offer Cumulative Organization Reports (CORs), employee metrics, and insights to lend another hand in assisting the growth of new and existing employees alike. ThirdEye wishes you luck in your endeavor.
